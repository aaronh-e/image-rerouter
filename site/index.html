Pilot results

Can a small model finish a big model's image?

An image model draws by removing noise over 30 steps. We let a large model (SDXL) and a small one (SSD-1B) split those steps, and measured how much GPU time each split saves and what it does to the picture.

How it works

Two models, one image, a handoff in the middle

Early denoising steps decide the layout: what goes where. Late steps add detail: texture, edges, lettering. A handoff gives some of those steps to the cheaper model.

The two models share the same internal image format, so the second one picks up exactly where the first stopped. No image is decoded or redrawn in between.
