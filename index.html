Can switching models halfway make AI image generation cheaper?

Diffusion models generate an image by starting from pure random noise and removing a little of it at each step. Our project uses 30 denoising steps. The early diffusion steps decide the image layout. The later diffusion steps refine the detail of the image. We had a big model and a smaller, faster one split the diffusion steps, with one model handing its in-progress image off to the other one partway through. Then we measured how much cheaper each handoff made an image, and how much image quality dropped as a result.

    Our methodology

    What are the 6 types of prompt?

    Composition. These prompts ask for a set number of objects in particular colours and positions, such as “three red cups to the left of a blue plate.” They test whether the model gets the layout right.

    Text. These prompts ask for specific words written in the image, such as a shop sign that says “OPEN 24 HOURS.” They test whether the words come out spelled correctly and readable.

    Hands & faces. These prompts are close-up photos of people, such as a hand holding an apple with all five fingers visible. Hands and faces are where image models most often make mistakes that people notice.

    Product. These prompts are realistic product photos, such as an espresso machine on a marble counter. They test clean surfaces, lighting and fine detail.

    Stylized. These prompts ask for a particular art style, such as a watercolour painting, pixel art or a woodblock print. They test whether the model keeps the style consistent across the whole image.

    Dense. These are long prompts that describe a busy scene with many objects, such as a cabin kitchen with a kettle, three bowls, a cat and a frosted window. They test whether the model includes everything the prompt asks for.
